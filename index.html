FAQ

Common seller questions

Do I need box and papers?

Not always. They can improve value, but many watches can still be reviewed without them depending on the model and condition.

What watches do you buy?

We focus on luxury and collectible models, especially brands with strong resale demand and a healthy secondary market.

How fast can I get a response?

You can position your business as fast-response once your lead handling is fully connected by email or text.

Can this work on GitHub Pages?
